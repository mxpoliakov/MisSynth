# Datasets
- [MisSynth.o4-mini.jsonl](MisSynth.o4-mini.jsonl), [MisSynth.gpt-5.jsonl](MisSynth.gpt-5.jsonl): the released synthetic datasets, generated by o4-mini and GPT-5 (medium) with K = 15 and M = 5. One record per MISSCI dev argument (30): the claim, accurate premise and retrieved article excerpt, 15 synthetic fallacies (`synthetic_fallacies`: context, fallacious premise, class) and 5 synthetic claim/premise pairs (`synthetic_claims_and_premises`).
- [mafalda.test.jsonl](mafalda.test.jsonl): MAFALDA in the MISSCI record format, 103 fallacies in 78 texts (`create_mafalda_dataset.py`).
- [logic.test.jsonl](logic.test.jsonl): Logic in the MISSCI record format, 1129 items (`create_logic_dataset.py`).
- [dev_split_A.txt](dev_split_A.txt), [dev_split_B.txt](dev_split_B.txt): the two halves of the MISSCI dev split used for configuration selection.

## Dev split
Rule: sort the 30 MISSCI dev arguments by (number of gold fallacies, descending; id) and
alternate A/B, so both halves have ~48 gold fallacies. One documented swap: arg-97 and
arg-137 share a verbatim gold fallacious premise in MISSCI, so arg-97 was moved to A and
arg-90 (same gold count) moved to B, keeping both copies on the training side.

Each dev argument cites a distinct publication, so an argument split is a source-article split.

A (15 arguments, 49 gold fallacies): training side for selection runs.
B (15 arguments, 47 gold fallacies): validation (gold fallacies only).
Final models are trained on all 30 arguments.
